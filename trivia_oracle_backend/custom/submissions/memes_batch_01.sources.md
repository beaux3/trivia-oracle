- Bonesmashing: striking one's own jaw/cheekbones with blunt objects on the belief it reshapes bone, dangerous, no reliable benefit: https://www.vice.com/en/article/gen-z-is-looksmaxxing-by-bonesmashing-their-faces/ ; https://www.healio.com/news/dermatology/20260622/qa-are-some-looksmaxxing-men-really-hitting-their-jaws-with-hammers
