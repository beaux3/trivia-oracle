# Sources for japanese_mythology_batch_01

Filed under the standard qbreader "Mythology" category rather than the custom "Japan" category,
since CONTRIBUTING.md reserves "Japan" for things not already covered by a qbreader category, and
world mythology (including Japanese mythology/folklore) is already covered by "Mythology."
Verification standard: one confirming source per fact.

## 1. Kappa
- Cucumber-loving, sumo-challenging water yokai; child-sized with a turtle shell, webbed hands/feet,
  beak, and a water-filled dish on its head that is the source of its strength: https://sakura.co/blog/the-japanese-kappa-mischevious-water-creature
- Legend of friendly kappa teaching humans medicine/bone-setting; kappamaki (cucumber roll) named
  after it: https://www.daiyokai.com/en/post/kappa-river-yokai-legend/

## 2. Amaterasu
- Born from Izanagi's left eye during his post-underworld purification; sun goddess ruling
  Takamagahara (the High Celestial Plain): https://www.britannica.com/topic/Amaterasu
- Susanoo's rampage (destroying rice fields, defiling her weaving hall) drives her into a cave,
  plunging the world into darkness; lured out by Ame-no-Uzume's ritual dance and her own
  reflection in a mirror; imperial family's claimed descent from her and the sacred mirror among
  the three imperial regalia: https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-topics/b11203/

## 3. Tanuki
- Bake-danuki shapeshifters dating to at least the Kamakura period; transformations into humans, objects (e.g. a teakettle); good-natured/sociable contrast with more dangerous kitsune: https://kamicrew.com/blog/tanuki-japanese-raccoon-dog-yokai/
- Statues outside businesses styled after the protective deity Jizo; 1994 Studio Ghibli film Pom Poko about tanuki using shapeshifting to resist suburban development: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pom_Poko
