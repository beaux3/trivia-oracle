# Anime batch 01 — clue sources and validation

71 custom anime questions: the original 20 anime-title tossups, the upstream Sasuke Uchiha question (21), 15 earlier anime-title additions (22–36), and 35 newly approved titles (37–71). The latest additions include 15 classic shonen titles and all 20 subsequently approved popular titles, with difficulty estimates of 3–5 and five reveal sentences each. Category and subcategory are `Anime`; alternate subcategory is `null`. Questions 1–21 are unchanged from upstream commit `cbf5cad`; questions 1–36 are unchanged by this latest append.

For questions 1–20, sources were opened during research on 27 September 2026, and each numbered verification item corresponds to one complete sentence with at least two independent sources. Questions 22–71 were researched on 28 September 2026 under the updated CONTRIBUTING.md requirement of at least one opened source per clue; their concise source notes follow sentence order. Question 21 and its source notes were retained from upstream. Research prioritizes official pages and encyclopedic episode guides; independently written episode analyses also corroborate scenes omitted by short official summaries. Publisher descriptions syndicated to different services are not counted as separate sources. These are original questions, not quotations or adaptations of existing tossups.

English and Japanese title forms are checked against the title references below. Punctuation, spacing, macron-free romanizations, and short initialisms are accepted as typing conveniences, not claimed as separate official titles. Initialisms include SAO, AoT/SnK, FMAB/FMA:B, PMMM, KnY, SxF, and S;G. SAO, AoT, and FMA:B are also documented at https://www.allacronyms.com/AoT/Attack_on_Titan.
