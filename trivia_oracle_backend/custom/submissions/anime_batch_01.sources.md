# Anime batch 01 — complete preview and clue sources

20 original anime-title tossups, with exactly two questions at each difficulty from 1 to 10. Each question has five reveal sentences; the concise length follows `singapore_batch_01.jsonl`. Category and subcategory are `Anime`; alternate subcategory is `null`.

Sources were opened during research on 27 September 2026. Each numbered verification item corresponds to one complete sentence of the question and includes at least two independent sources. Research prioritizes official pages and encyclopedic episode guides; independently written episode analyses also corroborate scenes omitted by short official summaries. Publisher descriptions syndicated to different services are not counted as separate sources. These are original questions, not quotations or adaptations of existing tossups.

English and Japanese title forms are checked against the title references below. Punctuation, spacing, macron-free romanizations, and short initialisms are accepted as typing conveniences, not claimed as separate official titles. Initialisms include SAO, AoT/SnK, FMAB/FMA:B, PMMM, KnY, SxF, and S;G. SAO, AoT, and FMA:B are also documented at https://www.allacronyms.com/AoT/Attack_on_Titan.

## Validation and limitations

- The requested validator passes: `anime_batch_01.jsonl: 20 question(s) are valid`.
- Exactly 20 distinct approved titles and 20 distinct answerlines; exactly two questions at each integer difficulty from 1 through 10.
- All 20 questions split into exactly five clues using the bot's actual `split_sentences()`; each final clue begins “For 10 points, name this”.
- All 92 intended title/alias inputs are accepted by the actual local `judge()`.
- All 380 pairwise checks against the other 19 canonical batch answers are rejected.
- Fifteen additional case, punctuation, spacing, and macron/romanization inputs are accepted.
- Five unqualified Fullmetal Alchemist forms prompt with “Which anime adaptation?”; three explicit 2003 forms are rejected.
- Three Steins;Gate 0 forms and Naruto Shippuden/Boruto forms are rejected; bare Madoka prompts for the anime title.
- Source coverage: 100 clue entries, each with two or more full source URLs; 84 distinct clue-source URLs.
- Validation used a temporary virtual environment with the backend requirements. No runtime database was loaded, no code or CONTRIBUTING.md was edited, and no commit was made.

Reproduce the format check from the repository root:

```sh
python -m trivia_oracle_backend.custom.add --check trivia_oracle_backend/custom/submissions/anime_batch_01.jsonl
```

Difficulty is an editorial estimate based on the lead and clue progression, not player-tested calibration. The final clues deliberately become accessible even at levels 8–10. Questions 1 and 2 use broadly shared television-series premises; the Spice and Wolf clues intentionally fit both television adaptations, and the Gintama question covers sequel seasons of the same television franchise. The Brotherhood lead and Ling/Greed clue exclude the 2003 continuity.

Verification uses published sources rather than a fresh viewing of every episode. Fine-grained scenes sometimes rely on independent episode analysis alongside an episode guide, and the sources are exposed above for spot-checking. Manga-titled encyclopedia pages are used only for facts also corroborated in anime-specific coverage. There are no knowingly unresolved factual contradictions in the retained clues.

Answer-matching results apply to the current **local** judge. The external qbreader judge used in API mode was not exercised, so its handling of aliases and prompts may differ. The existing local typo tolerance is unchanged: these checks establish the listed accepts, prompts, and rejections, not exhaustive rejection of every possible near-miss. Initialisms and punctuation variants are intentionally permissive; they are listed explicitly above.
