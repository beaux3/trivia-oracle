## 1. Serangoon (3)
- Name etymology (Saranggong/ranggong bird; serang dengan gong): https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/little-india-a-cultural-and-historical-precinct/story ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serangoon
- Tamil Muslim pioneers; Soonambu Kambam lime pits/brick kilns 1820s-1860: https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/little-india-a-cultural-and-historical-precinct/story
- Cattle/buffalo industry 1860s-1930s, Kandang Kerbau: https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/little-india-a-cultural-and-historical-precinct/story
- HDB built Serangoon circa 1981-1983 as one of seven new towns (with Bukit Batok/Gombak, Jurong East/West, Tampines, Woodlands, Hougang): https://www.hdb.gov.sg/cs/infoweb/about-us/history/hdb-towns-your-home/serangoon (per search summary); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serangoon
- Circle Line + North East Line interchange station: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serangoon_MRT_station ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serangoon
- Rooftop dog park and library above NEX (built on the MRT interchange): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serangoon ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nex,_Singapore

## 2. NEX (3)
- Gold Ridge Pte Ltd incorporated March 2008, won 2008 government tender for the site: https://recordowl.com/company/gold-ridge-pte-ltd ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nex,_Singapore
- Architect SAA Architects, contractor Low Keng Hua, ~600,000 sq ft, soft opening 25 Nov 2010, official opening 21 Apr 2011: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nex,_Singapore
- Serangoon Public Library opened 11 March 2011 on Level 4R of NEX, first public library in Singapore on a rooftop; rooftop dog park with wet/dry play areas: https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/visit-us/our-libraries-and-locations/libraries/Serangoon-Public-Library (per search summary) ; http://ramblinglibrarian.blogspot.com/2011/03/nlb-media-release-serangoon-public.html (quotes NLB's own media release)
- Anchor tenants (Shaw Theatres, FairPrice Xtra, Food Junction, Food Republic): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nex,_Singapore
- Built above Circle Line platforms of Serangoon MRT interchange, connects to bus interchange in basement: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nex,_Singapore ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serangoon_MRT_station

## 5. Telok Ayer (4)
- "Water bay" etymology; 1822 Raffles town plan Chinese district: https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/telok-ayer/story
- Landing point for immigrants; shrines along waterfront: https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/telok-ayer/story
- Land reclamation from 1880s using soil from Mount Wallich; Robinson Road/Cecil Street/Shenton Way built on reclaimed land: https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/telok-ayer/story ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thian_Hock_Keng
- Six National Monuments (Thian Hock Keng, Yu Huang Gong, Ying Fo Fui Kun, Al-Abrar Mosque, Nagore Dargah, Telok Ayer Methodist Church): https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/telok-ayer/story
- Thian Hock Keng rebuilt 1839-1842, funded by junk owners/sailors, dedicated to Mazu: https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/telok-ayer/story ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thian_Hock_Keng

- Founded March 1991 by Lincoln Cheng, inspired by Ibiza: https://thesmartlocal.com/read/zouk-singapore/ ; https://www.roots.gov.sg/Collection-Landing/listing/1191107
- Original site: three 1919 godowns on Jiak Kim Street, restoration details: https://www.roots.gov.sg/Collection-Landing/listing/1191107 ; https://thesmartlocal.com/read/zouk-singapore/
- Velvet Underground (1994) and Phuture (1996) rooms: https://www.roots.gov.sg/Collection-Landing/listing/1191107
- First club to push house music over Top 40; moved to Clarke Quay 2016 after 25 years: https://zoukgroup.com/singapore/history/ ; https://thesmartlocal.com/read/zouk-singapore/
