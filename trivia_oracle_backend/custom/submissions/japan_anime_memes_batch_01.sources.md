- 1999 child pornography statute criminalizing production/distribution of real CSAM; 2014 amendment criminalizing possession while explicitly exempting fictional/drawn depictions; international legal criticism of that exemption: https://digitalcommons.law.uga.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=2257&context=gjicl

## 47. Is that a JoJo reference

## 48. Maneki-neko
- Gotokuji Temple (Setagaya, Tokyo) origin legend: early-17th-century temple cat beckoning Ii Naotaka in from a thunderstorm, leading to the temple's renaming after his posthumous Buddhist name: https://www.jrailpass.com/blog/gotokuji-temple-tokyo
- Post-cat's-death "Beckoning Cat Hall" with thousands of white cat statues: https://www.washingtonpost.com/travel/2024/01/08/lucky-cats-maneki-neko-japan-temple-gotokuji/
